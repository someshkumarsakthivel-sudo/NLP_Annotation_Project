# Annotation Guidelines

## Intent

REFUND_REQUEST
Use when the customer asks for a refund or wants their money back.

DELIVERY_ISSUE
Use when the customer asks about delivery, tracking, arrival, or a late order.

PAYMENT_ISSUE
Use when the customer reports a payment problem or an incorrect charge.

ACCOUNT_ISSUE
Use when the customer has a problem with their account, password, or login.

PRODUCT_ISSUE
Use when the customer reports a problem with a product or mentions a specific product issue.

GENERAL_QUERY
Use when the message does not match the other intent categories.

## Sentiment

POSITIVE
Use when the customer expresses happiness, satisfaction, or appreciation.

NEGATIVE
Use when the customer expresses anger, disappointment, dissatisfaction, or a complaint.

NEUTRAL
Use when the customer is asking a normal question or does not clearly express positive or negative emotion.

## Priority

HIGH
Use when the issue is urgent or needs immediate attention.

MEDIUM
Use when the customer has a problem that needs attention but is not urgent.

LOW
Use for normal questions or requests that do not need immediate attention.

## Entities

ORDER_ID
Identify order numbers such as #A10245.

AMOUNT
Identify money values such as ₹2,500.

LOCATION
Identify locations such as Chennai or Coimbatore.

PRODUCT
Identify products such as phone, laptop, headphones, tablet, or watch.

## Quality Check

Check whether the assigned labels match the annotation guidelines.

If the annotation follows the guidelines, mark it as PASS.

If the annotation does not follow the guidelines, mark it as FAIL.
